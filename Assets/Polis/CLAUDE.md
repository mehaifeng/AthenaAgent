# Game Mode ("Athena's Polis")

This file is loaded on demand: Claude Code reads it when it opens a file under `Assets/Polis/`, and the `CLAUDE.md` files in `Services/GameMode/`, `ViewModels/GameMode/`, `Views/GameMode/` and `Tools/GameMode/` import it. Other agents: the root `AGENTS.md` points here. It was split out of the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-12 because it was a quarter of that file and is only needed when touching game mode.

The center area switches between the conversation and a game in which the workspace is a 2.5D city (`MainLayoutSettings.GameMode`; the `GameModeToggleButton` sits in the conversation header beside the right-panel toggle; only the user switches — selecting a session never leaves game mode). In game mode the game covers everything below the header, the composer row included (`PolisView` spans both rows, `ComposerHost` is hidden rather than merely covered, so neither focus nor its Enter binding can land in an invisible box); you talk to Athena in the city's talk bar. Design, decisions and evidence (the M2 art review included): `Docs/GameMode_Design_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_Progress_CN.md`; manual acceptance: `Docs/GameMode_M1_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M2_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M3_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M4_Acceptance_CN.md` (citizens onwards); external models: `Docs/GameMode_AssetManifest_CN.md`; the fourth round (townsfolk from buildings, day and night, trades): `Docs/GameMode_M6_Plan_CN.md` and the in-game checklist `Docs/GameMode_M6_Checklist_CN.md`.

## Load-bearing rules, each held by a test

- **Athena does not mirror turns** (2026-10-13, the user's decision; it reverses the design's section 6). No tool call, reasoning or approval event reaches the page: the orchestrator, the replay, the narration library (`Polis.Line.*`), the live event feed (`live.mjs`'s `events` / `focus`), `PolisEventProjector`, `PolisReplayConverter`, the maidens that flew out on a sub-agent dispatch and the scaffolds are all deleted. The reason is measured, not taste: `Tools/GameMode/rhythm/rhythm.py` over the dev logs (198 turns, 2026-07-23..10-12) puts tool execution at a 0.05 s median, 30 % of tool starts within 0.5 s of the previous one, 40 % of turns with no tool call at all, and model waits at a 4.7 s median — no animation clock fits a per-call mirror, and a mirror offers the player no choice. What is left of a turn in the city: the talk bar (busy, "ask her to stop", and a **red seal while an approval waits** — the one place the city shows it; the approval itself stays in the native window) and the item, which **Athena runs to hand you the moment the bubble stops streaming** (2026-10-13, the user: when a task is done she runs to you and hands over the scroll, following you while you move until you stop; the page treats any new pending item that arrives after the city's first item list as fresh; items present at load wait on the agora's south row). The hand-over (`HANDOVER`, `stepHandover`): fresh items queue up and are not in the city until handed (`awaitingHandover`); she runs (`athena.chase`) carrying the scroll to the standable point on a 1 m ring round you nearest her (`handoverTarget` — straight toward her can be the sea, and the first version stood inside you), re-routes from where she is whenever that point drifts 0.8 m (checked every 350 ms), and once she is within 1.6 m and you have stood still 400 ms she gives it (`deliver`, 1.8 s, you `receive`); at 60 % it lands at your feet, and if you walk off before that she chases again. She is held (no wander) while handing over. Athena now lives in the city (`athena.mjs`, pure, `athena.test.mjs`): idle on the agora ring, a walk to the next of its six points every 6–10 s (seeded), every walk completes, **held** (no new wander) while you are within 2.5 m or talking to her. **She does not teleport and is not summoned** (2026-10-13, the user): a destination within `maxWalkMs` (10 s) is walked, anything farther is run (`run`, `runFactor` 0.45 of the walking time, ≈ 7.1 m/s, the `run` pose with a longer stride), and a word said far away no longer brings her — to talk to her you walk up and press E. The one exception to "every move completes" is `chase` (rule 6, item delivery): she runs from the point she is at (`pt:x,z`, computed by the page) and drops whatever was queued. She has no light at all now (`pose.teleport` is always null and `glow` 0; the fields stay so old reads do not break). `athena.test.mjs` holds walk/run, no teleport step, and chase's re-routing. The harbour boat sails on its own timetable (`BOAT_TRIP`, every 180 s for 20 s, by wall clock), so the sailor still stops hauling while it is out. C# keeps two things from the render model in `PolisDeliveries`: the delivery (bubble stopped streaming → `CreateItem`) and this turn's tool targets for external-change attribution — dropping the tool-status subscription would put every file she writes on the notice board. Held by `polis deliveries` / `polis tool targets` (Archive), `TestGameModeCityLifecycle` (no `events`/`focus` posted, the touch is recorded before the write, the seal on and off, the item pushed), smoke `existingItemWaitsOnTheRow` / `freshItemHandedByAthena` / `approvalOnlyOnTheBar` / `noLiveMirror` / `athenaIsNotSummonedFromAfar`, and `page.test.mjs` (no page module imports the deleted ones or matches tool / reasoning event names).
- **Townsfolk live in the buildings, not in the conversations** (fourth round, 2026-10-15, the user: a city of few conversations looked like a ghost town; the plan is `Docs/GameMode_M6_Plan_CN.md`). `PolisCitizens.Derive(cityKey, dwellings)` (pure, `Services/GameMode/`) settles every dwelling of the city — each folder building, each market stall's folder, and each cottage (below) — with **1–4 people fixed when the city loads**: size class 1 → 1–2, 2 → 2–3, 3–4 → 3–4, a stall or a ruin → 1 (`HeadCount`, by seed). Nothing about a person is saved and nothing comes from a conversation: the id is `n:<city 8 hex>:<person 12 hex>` from the SHA-256 of the city key, the place (`PlaceKey`: the plot, or `market/<folder>` for a stall) and the index in the dwelling, so the same folder on the same plot gives the same people every visit and a folder renamed in place keeps them (the ledger keeps its plot); the seed is the first four bytes little-endian, its lowest bit the sex (the page's `art/citizens.mjs` uses the same bit), the age band `(seed >> 12) % 10`. **The population has no cap; the street has one**: at most 32 (`StreetCap`, the user's number) are out, chosen round-robin over dwellings by most recent `LastModified`, so a big city shows one or two from every busy building instead of a whole family from a single one; everyone else is `indoors` (posted as such, ≤ 400, `PolisAccumulation.MaxIndoors`) and can be knocked for. Night owls (`(seed >> 17) % 8 == 0`, at most 4, street citizens only) are the few who go out at night. `citizens` carries `{ id, seed, female, name, home, age, trade, tradeName, temper, homeName, stall, cottage, nightOwl }` and `census { total, street, indoors }`. **Names are whole Greek names, unique within a city**: `AssignNames` walks the people in dwelling order (`DwellingOrder`, then `Index`) and gives each the seed's preferred index into its sex's list, else the next free one by a seeded stride coprime with the list; the lists (`Polis.Citizen.NamesMale` / `NamesFemale`) are exactly `NamesPerSex` (120) each in both locales, aligned by index, with a numbered second round (`Polis.Citizen.NameNth`) past 120. **Every trade comes from a fixed table** (`PolisTrades`, see "Citizens work at crafts"), picked by seed and biased by the building's kind; a stall's keeper is always a merchant. The card (`citizenInfoLines`) shows trade, age band, temperament, where he lives (the building's name, 「市集的摊位」, a cottage's family name) and the census line — no conversation, no title, no transcript (`citizen-transcript`, the steles and the elders are deleted with the old derivation). **Looks are the citizens' own vocabulary** (`citizenLook`): garment (no chlamys), five cloth tones, five `plain*` hairstyles in `rig.HAIR_STYLES`, headband or none, beard, height, build, face — never a resident's signature (wide hat, felt cap, diadem, veil, sakkos, Athena's fillet, or any resident's trade tool), because residents are recognised at a distance by exactly those; a quarter carry a plain scroll, and each also carries the tools of his own craft, shown only while he works; no red. Any 32 consecutive seeds give ≥ 24 distinct looks. **Days run on the wall clock** (`citizens.mjs`, pure): a 150 s period — 45 s at home, a walk of ≤ 30 s at 1.3 m/s to one destination (agora ring, market, harbour, temple, library, grove, the fields or the pasture, or a neighbour's door), 25 s there, back (a citizen whose home has a door also spends 15 s inside at each end of the period and goes in and out through it — see "Doors citizens use"). **Standing still is taken off a citizen's own schedule**: while his card is open, he is being talked to, or he is in the debate's audience, his schedule clock (`scheduleClock` / `holdSchedule` / `resumeSchedule`) stops, and on release it goes on from the moment he stopped. **A route is accepted only if every 0.1 m of it is walkable** (`routeClear`; at 0.25 m a leg grazing a wall corner diagonally slipped between two samples once the northern plot row was walked). **Avoidance** (`separate`): two people closer than 0.6 m push apart along their line, ≤ 3 cm a frame, never onto unwalkable ground; Athena and the ten residents stand and are never pushed; **you are the stone** — anyone within 0.7 m of you steps aside. **Tiers decide the frame rate** (`assignTiers`, citizens and the ten residents together; Athena and you are always full): A = the nearest four *and* within 6 m — cloth simulated, fingers and toes shown; B = the rest in view — cloth frozen, fingers and toes hidden; C = out of view — not posed, not drawn; 1 m hysteresis. Measured (headless 1600×1000, M-B): the real 24-building city went from 12.6 fps with 32 citizens before tiers to 37.2 (Chromium) / 25.8 (WebKit). **A figure's first pose resettles its cloth** (`rig.mjs`: `stale` when `state.clock` is null), or the first simulated frame drags the skirt in from where the figure was built. **Small talk is rare and the model's alone**: the page picks one pair in the whole city at a time (both idle with at least `chatNeedMs` (8 s) left, within 2.5 m, in view and within 15 m of you, not the one you are talking to, the pair not heard in 3 min, and `relations.canChat`) and sends `ambient-chat` `{ a, b, states }`; C# (`AmbientChatAsync`) streams A's opening line, then B's reply on the `Ambient` pool (60 an hour), one pair at a time, and ends with `ambient-end`; on no model, busy, limited or failed nobody says anything (`nextChatAfter`: no model — not again in this city; limited — 5 min; busy — 30 s; failed — 1 min). A greeting when you come within 2.5 m of an idle citizen (once per 2 min) is local (`Polis.Citizen.Greet*`). Held by `polis citizens` (Archive: head counts by size and kind, ids and seeds stable across calls and different per city, a renamed folder keeps its people, no street cap on the population and exactly 32 out, round-robin over buildings, night owls ≤ 4 and only on the street, 300 people get 300 names, stalls are merchants, trades always known), `TestGameModeCitizens` (headless: 20 folders → a census above 32 with exactly 32 on the street, every card line and payload free of title and message markers, nothing citizen-shaped in `polis.json`), `citizens.test.mjs`, smoke `citizensCheck` (the cap, the card) and the motion self-test (labels and bubbles against the frame's projection).
- **Townsfolk are out by day and home by night** (fourth round, need 4, 2026-10-15, the user: they go out at sunrise and come home in the evening, following the app's theme; at night a scattered few go out, and what they say differs). The sky is the app's theme ("Night follows the app's theme" below), and the page keeps `sky { night, since, after }` (`setNight`: `since` is when it last changed, null if the page opened that way; at dawn `after` is when night fell). `citizens.mjs` rule 8: **nobody vanishes on the spot**. At dusk a citizen who is not a night owl finishes his round and stays in the next time he gets home (`retireTime`: a home with a door — the end of the walk-in leg; a doorless home — the next homecoming to the point in front of it, up to two periods, because the first homecoming may be the one he is on right now; a doorless home with no reachable destination never retires — he stands at his door all day and would otherwise just disappear); indoors he is not drawn (`indoorsAt`, `asleepAt`). A city opened at night starts with only the owls out. At dawn each comes out at **his own** next going-out time (`wakeTime`), so the street fills over a period instead of all at once. Night owls keep their day. **The moment is in every prompt**: the page sends `where` / `doing` with a chat and per person with a chorus or an ambient chat (`stateOf` / `statesOf`; C# accepts only `PolisTalk.WhereKeys` — home, inside, agora, market, harbor, temple, library, grove, fields, pasture, neighbour, bema, road — and `DoingKeys` — work, walk, idle, chat, audience, stroll — and drops anything else), and `PolisMoment` adds the local time and day or night (`MomentText`); the rules say at night that it is late and lines are quieter (`RulesText(…, night)`). - `Services/GameMode/`: the polis as pure functions — `PolisScanner` (bounded, cancellable, skips hidden and `GeneratedDirectories`), `PolisBuildings`, `PolisLayoutLedger` (plots never move; `Rename` keeps a renamed folder's plot), `PolisToolCategories` (the one tool → action table; `SubAgentZones` derives from it), `PolisPaths`, `PolisSave` / `PolisSaveStore` (atomic per-workspace saves with per-record quarantine), `PolisDiff` (offline renames, item recovery), `PolisChanges` (attribution, batching), `PolisIntents` (the closed page-intent set), `PolisCityBuilder`, `PolisFixture` (the city snapshot; no replay since 2026-10-13), `PolisToolTargets` (a tool call's path → place, for attribution and deliveries), `PolisCitizens` (the townsfolk settled in each dwelling), `PolisTrades` (the trade table), `PolisHouseholds` (cottages and their plots), `PolisBriefs` (a building's and the project's brief, bounded and confined to the workspace), `PolisTalk` / `PolisTalkService` (how the city's people talk: persona, world, moment, prompt, the streamed call), `PolisDebate` / `PolisDebateService` / `PolisContestStore` (the debate, its two model roles and the global record) — the two services are the only game types that call a model; `PolisRelations` / `PolisRelationStore` (how people stand with you and with each other, and the global ledger of what happened); M2: `PolisCollection` (venues and pedestals), `PolisCommission` / `PolisGrove`, `PolisResidents`, `PolisSuggestions`, `PolisHerald`, `PolisIslands`, `PolisOracle`, `PolisAssetManifest` / `PolisAssetStore`. No Avalonia types; `Athena.Archive.Tests` links the whole folder.
- `ViewModels/GameMode/`: `GameModeViewModel` (mode, page lifecycle and its four failure points, city loading, external changes, items, intents), `PolisDeliveries` (deliveries and this turn's tool targets from the render model), `PolisLocale` (the page's locale tables), `PolisAccumulation` (projects sessions, cron tasks and the save into the grove, residents, citizens, board, oracle, chart and herald payloads), `IPolisShell` (what the game may ask the shell to do; `MainWindowViewModel` implements it).
- `Views/GameMode/PolisView` hosts the game page (WebView created lazily through `Controls/PolisPage.cs`, failure panel underneath).
