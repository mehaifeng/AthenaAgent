# Game Mode ("Athena's Polis")

This file is loaded on demand: Claude Code reads it when it opens a file under `Assets/Polis/`, and the `CLAUDE.md` files in `Services/GameMode/`, `ViewModels/GameMode/`, `Views/GameMode/` and `Tools/GameMode/` import it. Other agents: the root `AGENTS.md` points here. It was split out of the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-12 because it was a quarter of that file and is only needed when touching game mode.

The center area switches between the conversation and a game in which the workspace is a 2.5D city (`MainLayoutSettings.GameMode`; the `GameModeToggleButton` sits in the conversation header beside the right-panel toggle; only the user switches — selecting a session never leaves game mode). In game mode the game covers everything below the header, the composer row included (`PolisView` spans both rows, `ComposerHost` is hidden rather than merely covered, so neither focus nor its Enter binding can land in an invisible box); you talk to Athena in the city's talk bar. Design, decisions and evidence (the M2 art review included): `Docs/GameMode_Design_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_Progress_CN.md`; manual acceptance: `Docs/GameMode_M1_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M2_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M3_Acceptance_CN.md`, `Docs/GameMode_M4_Acceptance_CN.md` (citizens onwards); external models: `Docs/GameMode_AssetManifest_CN.md`.

## Load-bearing rules, each held by a test

- **Athena does not mirror turns** (2026-10-13, the user's decision; it reverses the design's section 6). No tool call, reasoning or approval event reaches the page: the orchestrator, the replay, the narration library (`Polis.Line.*`), the live event feed (`live.mjs`'s `events` / `focus`), `PolisEventProjector`, `PolisReplayConverter`, the maidens that flew out on a sub-agent dispatch and the scaffolds are all deleted. The reason is measured, not taste: `Tools/GameMode/rhythm/rhythm.py` over the dev logs (198 turns, 2026-07-23..10-12) puts tool execution at a 0.05 s median, 30 % of tool starts within 0.5 s of the previous one, 40 % of turns with no tool call at all, and model waits at a 4.7 s median — no animation clock fits a per-call mirror, and a mirror offers the player no choice. What is left of a turn in the city: the talk bar (busy, "ask her to stop", and a **red seal while an approval waits** — the one place the city shows it; the approval itself stays in the native window) and the item, which **Athena runs to hand you the moment the bubble stops streaming** (2026-10-13, the user: when a task is done she runs to you and hands over the scroll, following you while you move until you stop; the page treats any new pending item that arrives after the city's first item list as fresh; items present at load wait on the agora's south row). The hand-over (`HANDOVER`, `stepHandover`): fresh items queue up and are not in the city until handed (`awaitingHandover`); she runs (`athena.chase`) carrying the scroll to the standable point on a 1 m ring round you nearest her (`handoverTarget` — straight toward her can be the sea, and the first version stood inside you), re-routes from where she is whenever that point drifts 0.8 m (checked every 350 ms), and once she is within 1.6 m and you have stood still 400 ms she gives it (`deliver`, 1.8 s, you `receive`); at 60 % it lands at your feet, and if you walk off before that she chases again. She is held (no wander) while handing over. Athena now lives in the city (`athena.mjs`, pure, `athena.test.mjs`): idle on the agora ring, a walk to the next of its six points every 6–10 s (seeded), every walk completes, **held** (no new wander) while you are within 2.5 m or talking to her. **She does not teleport and is not summoned** (2026-10-13, the user): a destination within `maxWalkMs` (10 s) is walked, anything farther is run (`run`, `runFactor` 0.45 of the walking time, ≈ 7.1 m/s, the `run` pose with a longer stride), and a word said far away no longer brings her — to talk to her you walk up and press E. The one exception to "every move completes" is `chase` (rule 6, item delivery): she runs from the point she is at (`pt:x,z`, computed by the page) and drops whatever was queued. She has no light at all now (`pose.teleport` is always null and `glow` 0; the fields stay so old reads do not break). `athena.test.mjs` holds walk/run, no teleport step, and chase's re-routing. The harbour boat sails on its own timetable (`BOAT_TRIP`, every 180 s for 20 s, by wall clock), so the sailor still stops hauling while it is out. C# keeps two things from the render model in `PolisDeliveries`: the delivery (bubble stopped streaming → `CreateItem`) and this turn's tool targets for external-change attribution — dropping the tool-status subscription would put every file she writes on the notice board. Held by `polis deliveries` / `polis tool targets` (Archive), `TestGameModeCityLifecycle` (no `events`/`focus` posted, the touch is recorded before the write, the seal on and off, the item pushed), smoke `existingItemWaitsOnTheRow` / `freshItemHandedByAthena` / `approvalOnlyOnTheBar` / `noLiveMirror` / `athenaIsNotSummonedFromAfar`, and `page.test.mjs` (no page module imports the deleted ones or matches tool / reasoning event names).
- **Every conversation is a citizen** (M-B, 2026-10-13; direction ① the user chose on 2026-10-12). `PolisCitizens.Derive` (pure, `Services/GameMode/`) turns each session of the city — the same workspace filter as the grove; the sanctuary's are the global chats — into one citizen: the seed is the first four bytes (little-endian) of the SHA-256 of the conversation id and its lowest bit is the sex (the page's `art/citizens.mjs` uses the same bit, so the name and the face agree); home is the top-level folder its successful tool calls touched most (`b:<folder>`, a market stall's folder lives at `market`), otherwise one of ten public places by seed; the epithet is the title's first word after fillers like 请帮我 (≤ 6 CJK, ≤ 12 Latin, none for a default title) and since 2026-10-13 only tells households which sessions are untitled — it is no longer part of a name; age bands by when it started (30 d / 180 d); **an elder** (not touched for a year, not running) never walks — his name is cut on a stele at his home (`world.setElders`, a 0.5-scale `prop.stele` that never blocks: it stands on the kerb by the home point, and blocking it would wall off a stand point). **At most 32 on the street** (`PolisCitizens.StreetCap`, the user's number), the most recently updated non-elders; the page enforces the same cap again (`TOWN.streetCap`) and builds at most two figures a frame. Citizens are **derived, never saved**: deleting a session removes its citizen at the next two-second accumulation pass, and nothing citizen-shaped is in `polis.json`. **Names are whole Greek names, unique within a city** (2026-10-13, the user: no title fragment glued to a Greek name; male and female names differ): `PolisCitizens.AssignNames` walks the city's citizens oldest conversation first and gives each the seed's preferred index into its sex's list, else the next free one by a seeded stride coprime with the list — so a newcomer never renames an older citizen, and only deleting an older citizen can hand a later one back its preferred name. The lists (`Polis.Citizen.NamesMale` / `NamesFemale`) are exactly `PolisCitizens.NamesPerSex` (120) each in both locales, aligned by index so switching language keeps the person, with no name in both; past 120 of one sex a second round starts with a number (`Polis.Citizen.NameNth`, zh `{0}（{1}）`). The Archive case checks 300 citizens get 300 names, a newcomer changes none, and both locale lists' length and uniqueness. **No message text reaches the citizens' payload**: names come from those lists, the card shows the title, outcome, counts and folder names; both the Archive and the headless case plant a marker string in user and assistant text and fail if it appears anywhere in the payload (it reaches the page only through the transcript you open, below). **Looks are the citizens' own vocabulary** (`citizenLook`): garment (no chlamys), five cloth tones, five `plain*` hairstyles in `rig.HAIR_STYLES`, headband or none, beard, height, build, face — never a resident's signature (wide hat, felt cap, diadem, veil, sakkos, Athena's fillet, or any trade tool), because residents are recognised at a distance by exactly those; a quarter carry a plain scroll; no red. Any 32 consecutive seeds give ≥ 24 distinct looks. **Days run on the wall clock** (`citizens.mjs`, pure): a 150 s period — 45 s at home, a walk of ≤ 30 s at 1.3 m/s to one destination (agora ring, market, harbour, temple, library, grove, or a neighbour's door), 25 s there, back. **Standing still is taken off a citizen's own schedule** (2026-10-13, the user: a walker stopped for a talk raced, after Esc, to where the wall clock said he should be): while his card is open, he is being asked, or he is in the debate's audience, his schedule clock (`scheduleClock` / `holdSchedule` / `resumeSchedule`, kept on the citizen and surviving re-plans) stops, and on release it goes on from the moment he stopped — a 600 ms glide had covered whatever distance the wall clock had walked meanwhile (over 2 m for a 20 s talk in the test). So "the same moment is the same place" holds only for someone never stopped. **A route is accepted only if every 0.25 m of it is walkable** (`routeClear`): the street graph's first leg runs straight from an agora stand point to a corner and crossed the bema and the altar (7 of 9 632 day samples were inside them before). **Avoidance** (`separate`): two people closer than 0.6 m push apart along their line, ≤ 3 cm a frame, never onto unwalkable ground; Athena and the ten residents stand and are never pushed; **you are the stone** — anyone within 0.7 m of you steps aside, so a citizen never blocks your way. **Tiers decide the frame rate** (`assignTiers`, citizens and the ten residents together; Athena and you are always full): A = the nearest four *and* within 6 m — cloth simulated, fingers and toes shown; B = the rest in view — cloth frozen (`style.cloth === false`, the skirt keeps its last root-local shape and moves with the body), fingers and toes hidden (`setDetail`); C = out of view — not posed, not drawn; 1 m hysteresis. Measured (headless 1600×1000): the real 24-building city went from 12.6 fps with 32 citizens before tiers to 37.2 (Chromium) / 25.8 (WebKit), and from 28.0 to 60.2 with none, because the residents are tiered too — a figure is 66 meshes / 46k triangles drawn four times a frame (shadow, main, GTAO, mask) and its cloth costs 2.3 ms a frame. **A figure's first pose resettles its cloth** (`rig.mjs`: `stale` is true when `state.clock` is null): figures are draped at the origin when built and moved to their spot afterwards, and the first simulated frame dragged the skirt in from there — four of a line of ten flung their skirts 0.6–1.1 m out (`citizens.test.mjs` reads 1.09 m on the old rule). **Small talk is rare, and since 2026-10-13 the model's alone** (the user: no preset lines between NPCs; while a reply comes the bubble loops dots, then the reply streams): the page picks — one pair in the whole city at a time, both idle with at least `chatNeedMs` (8 s) left, within 2.5 m of each other, in view and within 15 m of you, not the citizen you are chatting with, the pair not heard in 3 min — and sends `ambient-chat` `{ a, b }` (both this city's citizens, never the same one; `PolisIntents`). The pair stands still while they talk (their schedule clocks are held, like a citizen you talk to) and faces each other; whoever's line is streaming gestures. C# (`AmbientChatAsync`) asks the model for A's opening line, streams it into A's bubble, posts an empty partial for B (dots at once), then asks for B's reply with A's line in it (`PolisTalk.BuildAmbientLine`: the world, both names and personas, and only **how far each one's commission got** (`AmbientCalling`) — **no title, date, folder or message text**, because nobody pressed Enter for this call; one sentence ≤ `MaxAmbientChars` (40), the UI language), both on the `Ambient` pool (60 calls an hour, the user's number — 30 conversations), one pair at a time in C# too (`_ambientRunning`), and ends with `ambient-end` `{ a, b, status }`. There are **no local lines to fall back on**: on no model, busy, limited or failed nobody says anything — the page drops the dots and waits (`nextChatAfter`: no model — not again in this city; limited — 5 min; busy — 30 s; failed — 1 min), and a pair that has talked for `chatMaxMs` (60 s) is let go regardless. Each `talk` carries its line's number (`n`) so a partial that arrives after its own final is dropped while the next line's partials are not. The citizen's old card lines (`PolisCitizens.Lines`, `Polis.Citizen.Line.*`) and the `Polis.Citizen.Chat.*` pairs are deleted; a greeting when you come within 2.5 m of an idle citizen (once per 2 min) is still local (`Polis.Citizen.Greet`). Labels show only within 6 m or when you can reach the citizen. The stoa's two hang **on the column line between the two columns nearest ±W/4, right under the architrave on a 0.62 m chain** — hung 0.6 m behind it (the previous version) a 45° azimuth put them behind a column and the entablature hid their lids. `wallLantern` (a bracket with a scroll strut) hangs beside house doors (on the side toward the middle of the house, clear of the windows), both sides of the treasury, archive and vault doors, and the store's door. *Street lamps* (`kit.lampPost`, placed by `world`'s `placeLampPosts`): a plot is 12.5 m and a door is ~3 m from the street edge, so door light never reached the crossings or the agora's corners; every crossing gets one bronze post on the first free corner (by hash; 0.45 m inside the plot, never on the street; the quay row only on its north corners; skipped where anything stands within 0.9 m), arm and lantern toward the crossing. The post's shaft blocks a small circle (`userData.footprint`); every lantern hangs above the body band (0.35–1.6 m), so none of them blocks. *Scattering* is a night-only glow pass in `render.mjs` (`GLOW`: before tone mapping, the part above 0.75 extracted at half resolution, blurred at 1/2–1/16 and added back, strength × `nightK`; disabled by day). Held by `art.test.mjs` (`night lamps: …` — every patch lands on today's `ShaderLib.standard` chunks and a missing chunk throws, the field is lit in front of a door and 0 behind it, a lantern lights evenly round itself, the height channel, houses carry a door light and a wall lantern, ruins none, the stoa's lanterns have horn panes, every lantern hangs above 1.6 m, the night ramp), `collision.test.mjs` (`street lamps: …` — a post on a corner of every crossing that has a free one, never on the street, blocking only itself, the crossing still walkable and lit, every building's lights in the map) and smoke `nightGlow`; removing the wall clip, the posts or a chunk each fails its check — while the overlay (`OVERLAY_PALETTES`), the voyage and the DOM (talk bar, toolbar, reader, via `body.night`) switch to night palettes. The view's own background follows too. `TestGameModeSwitchAndFailures` flips the Avalonia theme and expects the message, smoke `nightFollowsTheme` the page.
- **The voyage** (`voyage.mjs`, canvas only, deterministic in `t`) is a layered scene — sky with sun or crescent moon and stars, drifting clouds, the departure island receding and the destination rising, a perspective sea with a glitter path, a Greek ship that bobs and pitches with a V wake and Athena's lamp aboard, gulls, a title card with a meander rule and a route line tracking progress; the camera pulls back 16 % while the city is still visible and settles in from 0.82× on arrival (`ARRIVAL`, 1.2 s ease-out).
- Page UI words and place names live in the locale files (`Polis.Ui.*`, `Polis.Place.*`, `Polis.Public.*`, M2's `Polis.Resident.*` / `Polis.Board.*` / `Polis.Oracle.*`, plus `GameMode.*` — the herald's lines are `GameMode.Herald.*` and `GameMode.Report.*`), pushed to the page on ready and on language change. Not done on purpose: restoring the last selected session at startup (design 15.5, left to the user).
- **M2 accumulation is either saved or derived, never both.** Save schema v3 adds item placements (`PolisPlacement`: venue + slot), met residents and the last visit; a broken placement only falls back to "kept, not placed" (`PolisSaveReadResult.RepairedPlacements`), never costing the item. Accepted items stand on the pedestals of three civic venues on the harbour row z = 2 — painted stoa (images), archive (documents, answers), vault (ledgers), 12 each (`PolisCollection`); the venue is the user's choice, a full venue is never extended, and a lost item stays as an empty pedestal with its name plate until the user removes it (`remove-item` is refused for anything not lost). The olive grove (one tree per completed commission, aged by its turns, `PolisGrove`) is re-derived from the conversation archive and never stored. **The ten residents are in every city from the start** (2026-10-13, the user: the tool-like roles lose their tool abilities but keep their trade, the business they look after, and a personality, and chat like citizens; the gold maiden is gone): `PolisResidents.All`, each with a fixed Greek name that is in neither citizen list (`Polis.Resident.*.GreekName` — surveyor Anaximandros, scribe Kadmos, accountant Pasion, smith Hephaistion, sailor Nearchos, librarian Zenodotos, herald Talthybios, painter Kalypso, artisan Daidalos, priestess Themistokleia; painter and priestess are women, `IsFemale` matching the page's `face.female`), a title of trade plus name (`Polis.Ui.ResidentTitle`, zh 「抄写员卡德摩斯」), an in-world intro (`.Intro`, no longer a capability pitch) and a portrait for the prompt (`.Persona`: what he looks after and his temperament). Their card offers "chat a little" (`resident-chat`) — the same lent bar, `chat` with `who: resident:<kind>` (`PolisIntents.ResidentSpeakerPrefix`, validated against `KnownResidentIds`), bubbles over their heads, the close-up on the two of you; the prompt is the world, his portrait, the rules without memory (`BuildChat` with `memory: null` — he entrusted nothing), and for everyone in town the rule that they only chat and never promise to do anything for Athena or anyone (nobody in the city can touch a tool). `try-resident` and the `.Try` prefills are deleted. `PolisResidents.Derive` (the tool → trade table, `dispatch_subagents` now under the artisan) still answers one question — has a capability been used (the board's memory suggestion reads the librarian) — and no longer decides who lives in the city. Only "met" is saved. The civic row is z = 2 because `GrowthOrder` only hands out z < 2, so no folder building is ever displaced.
- **Board, herald and chart are read-only projections, and every action they offer goes through the shell.** `PolisSuggestions` makes three local suggestions from folder names, counts and ages (no model call, no file contents), stable within a day; `PolisHerald` composes the away report on arrival (this workspace's cron results since `LastVisitedAt`, interrupted commissions, outside changes, the oracle's scroll); `PolisIslands` orders islands by workspace creation around the sanctuary and lights the ones with a running session. Taking a suggestion creates a commission through `IPolisShell.CreateCommissionAsync` and **prefills, never sends** (the talk bar opens with the text); `PrefillInput` takes a conversation id because the shell swaps its displayed chat only on beat two, so prefilling "the current composer" right after a selection wrote into the outgoing session. Picking an island selects that workspace's most recent session (or creates one) — the session tree's own path.
- **The oracle is the approval.** The temple's daily oracle opens a commission prefilled with a request for Athena to call `create_task` (`0 8 * * *`, named 神谕/Oracle, carrying the workspace id). The game holds only `ICronTaskReader`, so there is structurally no path from the page or `GameModeViewModel` to creating a task, and `create_task` is `IsNeverUnattended`: the native approval prompt is the ritual in every approval mode, Automatic included. `PolisOracle` recognises the task by workspace id and name; its runs arrive through the herald as scroll items.
- **The art is a code library** (`Assets/Polis/src/art/`): a building kit (`kit.mjs`; six types × subtypes × states in `buildings.mjs`), civic buildings, props, plants, rigged figures driven by pure pose functions (`poses.mjs`: walk, run, idle with fidgets, work variants, deliver, talk, wave, receive — Athena glided at 12 m/s through M2 and walks at 3.2 m/s since M3, the user having called the glide a ghost floating over the ground; the walk cap went from 5 s to 10 s so a trip up to ~32 m is walked and anything farther is the teleport — now `athena.ATHENA.maxWalkMs`), characters, and SVG busts (`busts.mjs`) drawn on canvas through `Path2D` — no DOM. Every asset has an id in `createAssetRegistry`; `AthenaData/Game/assets/manifest.json` can swap any one for a glTF (`PolisAssetManifest` validates in C#, `PolisAssetStore` resolves a file inside the folder literally and through `TryResolveFully`, `/polis-assets/` serves it), and every failure layer falls back to the procedural asset (`asset-manifest.mjs`). A figure entry may add `"rig": "procedural"` (figures only, never together with `animations`, rejected in C# and on the page): `art/bind.mjs` then skins the model onto that figure's own procedural skeleton, so every procedural action, the 220 ms blends, the look-at, the walk phase by distance and the held props (the red gift and seal included) work on it unchanged — the route for text-to-3D figures, which arrive static (2026-10-12; the first, Athena, is 500k triangles and 3 × 4096 textures out of the generator, 60k / 2048 after `gltf-transform` weld + simplify + resize). The model must stand in an A-pose with daylight under both armpits, or binding throws and the procedural figure stays. Landmarks come from horizontal slices of the *triangles*, not of the vertices (a decimated mesh has large flat triangles, and vertex slices cut one skirt into three pieces); the wrist is the narrowest slice within ±6 cm of a proportional guess (fingertip back 0.108 H along the arm — a tilted hand cut by horizontal planes is narrow at the fingertips too, which put the first version's wrist in the palm of the test mannequin); the shoulder joint sits 5 cm inside the torso's outer edge, not on the extrapolated arm axis (an A-pose elbow is usually bent, and the forearm-dominated axis landed 11 cm inside the shoulder). The dress below the waist is shared between the two thighs across its whole width (±18 cm), not ±7 cm around the midline — the narrow split creased the front panel on every step. The procedural body, its cloth and its spring bones are dropped (`dropBody`), so the hem no longer simulates, and an arm raised far above the shoulder still creases at the armpit (linear blend skinning; 12 smoothing passes made it a wedge instead of a spike). Embedded GLB textures need `connect-src blob:` in the page's CSP, in both the `<meta>` and `PolisAssetRoute.ContentSecurityPolicy`: GLTFLoader reads them with `fetch(blob:)`, and without it every textured external model rendered white in WebKit and Chromium alike — the smoke test's box carried no texture, so nothing caught it until it did. Held by `bind.test.mjs` (a synthetic A-pose mannequin, `mannequin.mjs`; four targeted mutations each caught), the smoke's `externalTextureLoaded` and `externalFigureOnProceduralRig`, and the extended `polis asset manifest` case; review a bound model with the gallery's `&manifest=<JSON>` (models from `/polis-assets/`) and `&weights=1` (vertices coloured by bone). Two colour rules survive a swap and are asserted: terracotta red is only on "your turn" things and only Athena's figure carries it (the scribe once delivered a red scroll), and an external figure floats the gift or seal at its chest from **whichever hand** the pose uses (`accentPropsShown`; the sealed tablet is in the left hand, and a right-hand-only check meant an external Athena never showed red while waiting for approval). Residents carry their trade's tool while walking and standing (`spec.carry`), and a held staff gets wrist compensation (`upright`) so it stands vertical. The art review renders every asset with `Tools/GameMode/web/test/gallery.mjs` into the ignored `Tools/GameMode/.local/m2-gallery/`; the per-asset findings and fixes are recorded in the progress file.
